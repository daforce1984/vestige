# 중력의 저편 — HOMEWORLD × GUNDAM (WebGPU real-time short film, 6:12, 24 fps)

Run: `python3 tools/serve.py 8791` → open http://localhost:8791/ in Chrome (WebGPU) → ▶ 재생 (sound on).
Keys: Space pause · ←/→ ±5 s · F fullscreen · H hide UI. URL params: `?t=120` start time, `&freeze=1`, `&debug=1` HUD,
`&scale=0.75` render scale, `&shutter=N` fixed motion-blur samples (default: adaptive 1–4).

## v2 — restyled after the EVE Online "Citadel" cinematic trailer
- Act I is a calm corporate propaganda ad ("Hiigaran Exodus Authority", narrator over a night-side Earth-like planet),
  hard-cut mid-sentence at 1:03 by the klaxon → fleet-commander radio chatter, fast cutting, walls of tracer fire,
  mass fleets (40+ capital ships), cold desaturated grade with orange fire, "FIND YOUR HOME. / BREAK THEIR WELL." end cards.
- 24 fps: film clock quantized to 1/24 s; each frame averages N sub-frames over a 180° shutter (real motion blur).
- Voices: 50 English lines, ElevenLabs v3 with audio tags (`assets/voice/lines.json` = script, timing, Korean subs).
  Regenerate: `uv run --with websocket-client tools/el_capture.py "<text>"` once (captures the signed-in page's request),
  then `tools/el_gen.py [ids]`. Cast: Eric (narrator), Adam (fleet command), Sarah (sensors), Liam (Sigma), Harry (strike lead).
- Music: MiniMax Music 3 via ComfyUI (`audio_minimax_music_3.json`, server :8188) — 6 cues in `assets/music/cues.json`,
  `tools/comfy_music.py [cue ids]`, stems scheduled from `assets/music/manifest.json` (falls back to the synthesized score).

## v3 — look pass (feedback: stars, grain, haze, readability; Star Trek 2009 debris-field reference)
- Ships redesigned in Blender after researching HW3 / The Expanse / Halo / Gundam UC references (`blender/DESIGN_NOTES.md`, `blender/shipkit.py`).
- Sparse small stars, grain ~off, low bloom/CA/god rays; cinematic key + camera-side fill + cool rim light; glossy hulls with
  analytic environment reflections and brushed-metal streaks.
- Explosions are billboards faked into volumes: domain-warped billowy density, self-shadowing toward the sun, blackbody core
  showing through soot pockets, heat-haze refraction sprites, refractive shock rings, tumbling hull chunks.
- Shutter samples never straddle a cut (fixes double exposure at edits).

## v4 — story rewrite + real assets
- Story simplified (SCRIPT_V3.md): home → trap → one pilot breaks the well → home. Reveals are slow Dune-style long takes with
  small ships in frame for scale (mothership arrival 16–40 s, dreadnought emergence 80–100 s), homecoming flight 340–356 s.
- Voices: 36 lines (12 new, ElevenLabs v3). Music: 6 epic cinematic cues (MiniMax Music 3, ComfyUI :8188).
- SFX: 43 Pixabay recordings (`assets/sfx_src` → `tools/build_sfx.py` → `assets/sfx`, credits in `assets/sfx/CREDITS.md`), 430 cues.
- Mechs: CC0 ATLAS/09 (hero) and RONIN/04 (enemy) by Ramon Linares, split into rigid parts (`blender/convert_mechs.py`).
- Earth: Solar System Scope 4k day/night/cloud maps (CC BY 4.0) in the sky shader.
- Gravitational lensing now bends only the background sky (fixes ships appearing twice); motion blur is reprojection-based.

## v9 — mace, crush deformation, launch DOF, baked mech paint
- Hero weapon is now a flanged war mace (`assets/mace.glb`, generated by `tools/make_mace.py`) held in a fist grip; the duel
  wrist poses are compressed to a natural cocked wrist (`maceWrist` in `js/duel.js`), and the contact solver re-fits every
  clash and the decisive blow (192.4 s lands on RONIN's torso, residual ≈ 4 mm).
- Mace blows crumple the target: world-space crush deformation in the mesh vertex shader (instance `crush` + `extra.yzw`,
  faceted normals + scorch in the dent), followed by sparks, bent plates, shock ring, shake. The berserk smash (273 story s)
  dents the gravity-well core, which keeps caving in until the implosion.
- Catapult launch (157.6–163): depth-of-field gather blur focused on the mech + stronger reprojection motion blur that
  spares the in-focus subject.
- Mechs: single UV atlas each + 2048² baked albedo/ORM (edge wear to bare silver metal, grime, streaks, scorch), visor recess fixed.
- Hangar: dark gunmetal PBR with box-projected interior reflections (no grid).
- Idle post-duel mech close-up removed (194.6–196.8 now shows the dreadnought before the lance charge).
- Hyperspace exits/entries take 1 s (homeland stretch → snap); mothership reveal is a far wide shot, slow push-in.
- Cold open is close and dense after the whip pan; during the well blast the spent hero drifts limp (no spin), seen from behind.

## v10 — new flagship, flank wound, Sigma
- New dark arrowhead flagship (`blender/ships_mothership_v9.py`); the dreadnought is scaled ×1.65 at load (`scale` model option).
- The enemy lance grazes the starboard flank: melt shader (`clipMin.w = 2`: noisy gouge, molten rim, drips) + vertex sag;
  a multi-deck interior cross-section (`assets/mother_bay.glb`) behind it; crates, cargo and crew spill out (`assets/bay_props.glb`).
- Dense models use a depth prepass (`prepass` model option; the prepass fragment runs the same cut-away discards) so early-Z
  works despite `discard` in the mesh shader.
- First-person tear: articulated mech hands with fingers (`tools/make_mech_hand.py`), camera aimed at the fists via duel FK.
- Hero mech is named SIGMA; it is recovered into the flagship's port launch bay before the jump home.

## Layout
- `SCRIPT.md` – original shot list / asset contract; `blender/` – procedural models → `assets/*.glb`
- `js/renderer.js`, `js/shaders.js` – raw WebGPU renderer (MSAA HDR, shadows, planet/nebula sky, lensing, bloom, grade, shutter accumulation)
- `js/world.js`, `js/shots.js`, `js/fx.js` – choreography as pure functions of film time (seek-safe)
- `js/audio*.js` – WebAudio: music stems + synthesized SFX/sync layer + voice playback (radio FX, ducking); master clock
- `tools/cdp.py` – drives the visible Chrome test tab (never opens tabs / headless browsers); `tools/el*.py` ElevenLabs tab

Memory: GPU resources allocated at init/resize only; stems decoded just-in-time and released after use.
