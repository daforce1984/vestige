# 중력의 저편 v3 — simple story, reveal-driven direction (6:12, 24 fps)

One line: *The exile fleet finally reaches home — a trap is waiting — one pilot breaks it — they go home.*

Direction rules
- Giant ships ENTER SLOWLY (8–15 s), always with small ships in frame for scale (Dune): escorts close to camera, the giant
  sliding in behind/above them, low angles, long lenses, no fast cuts during reveals, deep braam on each reveal beat.
- Fast cutting only in the battle (120–200). Reveals and emotional beats are long takes.
- Few speaking roles: NARRATOR (propaganda/epilogue), CMDR, SENSOR, PILOT (Sigma), WING.
